# Trademark policy

There is no trademark on the name of this format.

"CVD Policy Format", "CVD Policy", the file name `cvd.json`, the path
`/.well-known/cvd.json` and the `security.txt` field `CVD-Policy` may be used
freely by anyone, for any implementation, without permission and without a
licence fee.

We will not register a trademark on the format name, and we will not assert one.

Specifically, you may:

- implement the format in any product, open source or commercial
- state that your product reads, writes or validates the CVD Policy Format
- fork the specification and publish your own version under a different name
- publish competing tools, generators and validators

The only thing we ask, and cannot enforce: if your document does not follow the
specification, do not call it a CVD Policy document. Incorrect claims about
conformity are what makes a small format like this unreliable.

The name and logo of Skalvar Technologies UG (haftungsbeschränkt) are not covered
by this document and remain the property of that company.
